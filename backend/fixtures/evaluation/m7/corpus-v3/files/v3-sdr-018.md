# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-018: Ismailia Courier Lines

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 29 January 2026 by Procurement.

Ismailia Courier Lines is a courier supplier based in Ismailia, Egypt, used since 2021.

## Findings

Risk tier: low. Professional indemnity cover: USD 1,950,000. The supplier processes staff names and phone numbers on NileTech's behalf.

## Decision

The supplier is approved. The next review is due in 24 months.
