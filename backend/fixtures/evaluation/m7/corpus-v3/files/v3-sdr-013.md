# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-013: Sharjah Badge Systems

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 10 February 2026 by Procurement.

Sharjah Badge Systems is a identity badge supplier based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, used since 2025.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 2,800,000. The supplier processes staff names and phone numbers on NileTech's behalf.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
