# Post-incident review INC-4119: invoice export API

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 1. Began 5 April 2026 at 12:40 and lasted 118 minutes.

## Impact

73 client accounts could not download monthly invoice files during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a database connection pool sized for the previous release.

## Follow-up

The Platform team owns the corrective action, due 19 April 2026.
