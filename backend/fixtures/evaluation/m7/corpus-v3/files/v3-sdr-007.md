# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-007: Sharjah Badge Systems

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 16 February 2026 by Procurement.

Sharjah Badge Systems is a identity badge supplier based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, used since 2020.

## Findings

Risk tier: low. Professional indemnity cover: USD 2,500,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is approved. The next review is due in 24 months.
