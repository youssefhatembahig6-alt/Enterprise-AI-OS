# Post-incident review INC-4105: document upload service

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 17 May 2026 at 02:50 and lasted 45 minutes.

## Impact

79 client accounts could not upload supporting documents during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a configuration flag enabled in production before its migration ran.

## Follow-up

The Data Services team owns the corrective action, due 24 May 2026.
