# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-003: Amman Translation House

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 15 June 2026 by Procurement.

Amman Translation House is a translation supplier based in Amman, Jordan, used since 2017.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 1,750,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
