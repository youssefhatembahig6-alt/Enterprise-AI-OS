# Post-incident review INC-4120: document upload service

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 3. Began 16 February 2026 at 00:35 and lasted 193 minutes.

## Impact

71 client accounts could not upload supporting documents during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a database connection pool sized for the previous release.

## Follow-up

The Platform team owns the corrective action, due 10 March 2026.
