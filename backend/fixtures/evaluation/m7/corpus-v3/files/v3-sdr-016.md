# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-016: Ismailia Courier Lines

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 18 April 2026 by Procurement.

Ismailia Courier Lines is a courier supplier based in Ismailia, Egypt, used since 2017.

## Findings

Risk tier: medium. Professional indemnity cover: USD 2,150,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is approved with conditions. The next review is due in 12 months.
