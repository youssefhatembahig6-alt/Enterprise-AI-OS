# Post-incident review INC-4116: report scheduler

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 27 May 2026 at 07:05 and lasted 205 minutes.

## Impact

89 client accounts could not receive scheduled dashboard emails during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a configuration flag enabled in production before its migration ran.

## Follow-up

The Site Reliability team owns the corrective action, due 19 June 2026.
