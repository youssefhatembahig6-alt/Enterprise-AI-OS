# Post-incident review INC-4111: document upload service

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 4 February 2026 at 15:20 and lasted 138 minutes.

## Impact

40 client accounts could not upload supporting documents during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a full disk on the message broker after log rotation stopped.

## Follow-up

The Identity team owns the corrective action, due 25 February 2026.
