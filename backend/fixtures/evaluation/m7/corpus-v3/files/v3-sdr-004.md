# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-004: Ismailia Courier Lines

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 3 May 2026 by Procurement.

Ismailia Courier Lines is a courier supplier based in Ismailia, Egypt, used since 2025.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 750,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
