# Post-incident review INC-4109: document upload service

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 3. Began 11 March 2026 at 01:50 and lasted 213 minutes.

## Impact

44 client accounts could not upload supporting documents during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a full disk on the message broker after log rotation stopped.

## Follow-up

The Site Reliability team owns the corrective action, due 26 March 2026.
