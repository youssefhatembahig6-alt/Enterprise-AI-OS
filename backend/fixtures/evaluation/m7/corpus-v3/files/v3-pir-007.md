# Post-incident review INC-4107: invoice export API

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 28 May 2026 at 01:05 and lasted 75 minutes.

## Impact

22 client accounts could not download monthly invoice files during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a database connection pool sized for the previous release.

## Follow-up

The Identity team owns the corrective action, due 16 June 2026.
