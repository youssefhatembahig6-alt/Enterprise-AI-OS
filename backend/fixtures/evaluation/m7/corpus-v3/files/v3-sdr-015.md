# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-015: Tanta Cable Assemblies

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 23 February 2026 by Procurement.

Tanta Cable Assemblies is a network cabling supplier based in Tanta, Egypt, used since 2024.

## Findings

Risk tier: medium. Professional indemnity cover: USD 1,200,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is approved with conditions. The next review is due in 12 months.
