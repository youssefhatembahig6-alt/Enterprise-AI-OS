# Post-incident review INC-4113: invoice export API

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 1. Began 25 April 2026 at 21:35 and lasted 172 minutes.

## Impact

27 client accounts could not download monthly invoice files during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by an expired TLS certificate on an internal load balancer.

## Follow-up

The Site Reliability team owns the corrective action, due 22 May 2026.
