# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-019: Amman Translation House

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 29 April 2026 by Procurement.

Amman Translation House is a translation supplier based in Amman, Jordan, used since 2025.

## Findings

Risk tier: low. Professional indemnity cover: USD 450,000. The supplier processes staff names and phone numbers on NileTech's behalf.

## Decision

The supplier is approved. The next review is due in 24 months.
