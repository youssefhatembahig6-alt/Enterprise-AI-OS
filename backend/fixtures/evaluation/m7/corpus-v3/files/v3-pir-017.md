# Post-incident review INC-4117: single sign-on gateway

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 3 June 2026 at 00:35 and lasted 100 minutes.

## Impact

87 client accounts could not sign in to the client portal during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a database connection pool sized for the previous release.

## Follow-up

The Platform team owns the corrective action, due 21 June 2026.
