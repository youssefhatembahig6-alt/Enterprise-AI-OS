# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-012: Muscat Catering Partners

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 16 March 2026 by Procurement.

Muscat Catering Partners is a office catering supplier based in Muscat, Oman, used since 2015.

Risk tier: low. Professional indemnity cover: USD 2,850,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is approved. The next review is due in 24 months.
