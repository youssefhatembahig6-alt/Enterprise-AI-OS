# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-006: Ismailia Courier Lines

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 30 April 2026 by Procurement.

Ismailia Courier Lines is a courier supplier based in Ismailia, Egypt, used since 2019.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 2,750,000. The supplier processes staff names and phone numbers on NileTech's behalf.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
