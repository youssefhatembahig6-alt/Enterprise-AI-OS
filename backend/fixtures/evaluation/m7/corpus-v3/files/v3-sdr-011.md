# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-011: Muscat Catering Partners

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 21 January 2026 by Procurement.

Muscat Catering Partners is a office catering supplier based in Muscat, Oman, used since 2016.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 1,900,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
