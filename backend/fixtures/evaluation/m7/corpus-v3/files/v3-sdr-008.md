# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-008: Amman Translation House

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 20 June 2026 by Procurement.

Amman Translation House is a translation supplier based in Amman, Jordan, used since 2017.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 2,050,000. The supplier processes staff names and phone numbers on NileTech's behalf.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
