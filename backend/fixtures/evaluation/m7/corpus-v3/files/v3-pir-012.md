# Post-incident review INC-4112: invoice export API

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 10 March 2026 at 15:50 and lasted 222 minutes.

## Impact

116 client accounts could not download monthly invoice files during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by an expired TLS certificate on an internal load balancer.

## Follow-up

The Site Reliability team owns the corrective action, due 17 March 2026.
