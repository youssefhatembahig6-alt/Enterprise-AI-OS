# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-001: Tanta Cable Assemblies

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 22 March 2026 by Procurement.

Tanta Cable Assemblies is a network cabling supplier based in Tanta, Egypt, used since 2024.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 1,600,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
