# Post-incident review INC-4101: report scheduler

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 1. Began 22 May 2026 at 07:40 and lasted 50 minutes.

## Impact

135 client accounts could not receive scheduled dashboard emails during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a database connection pool sized for the previous release.

## Follow-up

The Identity team owns the corrective action, due 11 June 2026.
