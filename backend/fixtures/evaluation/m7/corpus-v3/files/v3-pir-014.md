# Post-incident review INC-4114: single sign-on gateway

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 3. Began 12 April 2026 at 01:50 and lasted 161 minutes.

## Impact

126 client accounts could not sign in to the client portal during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a configuration flag enabled in production before its migration ran.

## Follow-up

The Site Reliability team owns the corrective action, due 9 May 2026.
