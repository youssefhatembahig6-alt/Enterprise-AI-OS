# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-017: Muscat Catering Partners

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 5 March 2026 by Procurement.

Muscat Catering Partners is a office catering supplier based in Muscat, Oman, used since 2024.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 950,000. The supplier processes staff names and phone numbers on NileTech's behalf.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
