# Post-incident review INC-4103: document upload service

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 3. Began 18 June 2026 at 06:20 and lasted 47 minutes.

## Impact

133 client accounts could not upload supporting documents during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a full disk on the message broker after log rotation stopped.

## Follow-up

The Data Services team owns the corrective action, due 9 July 2026.
