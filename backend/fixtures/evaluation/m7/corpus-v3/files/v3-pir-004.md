# Post-incident review INC-4104: document upload service

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 1. Began 24 April 2026 at 12:00 and lasted 32 minutes.

## Impact

140 client accounts could not upload supporting documents during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a full disk on the message broker after log rotation stopped.

## Follow-up

The Site Reliability team owns the corrective action, due 1 May 2026.
