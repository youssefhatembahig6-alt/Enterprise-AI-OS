# Post-incident review INC-4118: single sign-on gateway

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 19 April 2026 at 14:05 and lasted 66 minutes.

## Impact

15 client accounts could not sign in to the client portal during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a full disk on the message broker after log rotation stopped.

## Follow-up

The Site Reliability team owns the corrective action, due 14 May 2026.
