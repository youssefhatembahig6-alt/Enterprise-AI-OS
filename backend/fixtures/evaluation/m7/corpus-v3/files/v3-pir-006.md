# Post-incident review INC-4106: invoice export API

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 1. Began 17 April 2026 at 04:00 and lasted 143 minutes.

## Impact

92 client accounts could not download monthly invoice files during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a database connection pool sized for the previous release.

## Follow-up

The Identity team owns the corrective action, due 16 May 2026.
