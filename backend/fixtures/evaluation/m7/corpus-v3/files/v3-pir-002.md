# Post-incident review INC-4102: report scheduler

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 2. Began 20 June 2026 at 15:05 and lasted 226 minutes.

## Impact

8 client accounts could not receive scheduled dashboard emails during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a configuration flag enabled in production before its migration ran.

## Follow-up

The Platform team owns the corrective action, due 11 July 2026.
