# Post-incident review INC-4115: invoice export API

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 3. Began 16 February 2026 at 16:50 and lasted 112 minutes.

## Impact

38 client accounts could not download monthly invoice files during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a configuration flag enabled in production before its migration ran.

## Follow-up

The Platform team owns the corrective action, due 6 March 2026.
