# Post-incident review INC-4110: invoice export API

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 3. Began 3 June 2026 at 00:00 and lasted 174 minutes.

## Impact

110 client accounts could not download monthly invoice files during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a configuration flag enabled in production before its migration ran.

## Follow-up

The Platform team owns the corrective action, due 22 June 2026.
