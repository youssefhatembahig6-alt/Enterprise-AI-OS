# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-020: Amman Translation House

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 10 February 2026 by Procurement.

Amman Translation House is a translation supplier based in Amman, Jordan, used since 2024.

## Findings

Risk tier: medium. Professional indemnity cover: USD 700,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is approved with conditions. The next review is due in 12 months.
