# Post-incident review INC-4108: document upload service

Synthetic M7 fixture. Severity 3. Began 1 May 2026 at 01:20 and lasted 105 minutes.

## Impact

62 client accounts could not upload supporting documents during the outage.

## Cause

The outage was caused by a database connection pool sized for the previous release.

## Follow-up

The Identity team owns the corrective action, due 9 May 2026.
