# Supplier due-diligence review SDR-009: Tanta Cable Assemblies

Synthetic M7 fixture. Reviewed 22 January 2026 by Procurement.

Tanta Cable Assemblies is a network cabling supplier based in Tanta, Egypt, used since 2015.

## Findings

Risk tier: high. Professional indemnity cover: USD 2,100,000. The supplier receives no personal data from NileTech.

## Decision

The supplier is suspended. The next review is due in 6 months.
