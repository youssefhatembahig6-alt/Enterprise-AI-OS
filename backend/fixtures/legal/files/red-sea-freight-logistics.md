# Logistics Services Agreement — Red Sea Freight

This Agreement is entered into by and between NileTech Solutions S.A.E. and Red Sea Freight Co. ("Provider").

## 1. Term

This Agreement commences on 1 July 2024 and expires on 30 June 2026, unless terminated earlier under section 3.

## 2. Fees and payment

Provider invoices monthly in arrears. NileTech shall pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

## 3. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 4. Limitation of liability

Provider's aggregate liability for loss of or damage to goods shall not exceed USD 250,000 per calendar year.

## 5. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
