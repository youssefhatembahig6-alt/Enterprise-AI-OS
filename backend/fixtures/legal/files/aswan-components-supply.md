# Master Supply Agreement — Aswan Components

This Agreement is made by and between NileTech Solutions S.A.E. ("NileTech") and Aswan Components Ltd. ("Supplier").

## 1. Term and renewal

This Agreement is effective from 1 April 2025 (the "Effective Date"). The initial term of this Agreement is twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date.

After the initial term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives at least ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Payment

NileTech shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days of receipt. Late amounts carry interest at 1% per month.

## 3. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving sixty (60) days' written notice to the other party. Either party may terminate immediately if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within 15 days.

## 4. Limitation of liability

Each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by NileTech in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret during this Agreement and for 3 years after it ends.

## 6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt.
