# IT security handbook

Synthetic fixture for NileTech staff.

## Passwords and MFA

All accounts require multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are never shared over chat or email.

## Laptops

Company laptops use full-disk encryption. Report a lost or stolen laptop to the IT service desk within one hour.

## Phishing

Forward suspicious emails to the security mailbox and do not click links. The security team runs a phishing simulation every quarter.
