# Annual leave policy 2026 🌴

Applies to all NileTech staff. Synthetic fixture. Marker NILETECH-INTERNAL-MARKER-2002.

## Entitlement

Employees in Egypt receive 21 working days of annual leave per year; after ten years of service this rises to 30 days. Employees in the UAE receive 30 calendar days.

Requests must be approved by the line manager.

| Country | Standard days | After 10 years |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Egypt (مصر) | 21 | 30 |
| UAE (الإمارات) | 30 | 30 |

## Carry-over

Up to five unused days may be carried into the first quarter of the next year. Carried days expire on 31 March. Requests must be approved by the line manager.

## Public holidays 🏖️

Public holidays follow the official calendar of each country, including Sham el-Nessim and Eid al-Fitr (عيد الفطر). Holidays that fall during approved leave are not deducted.

## Family leave 👨‍👩‍👧

Parents may take ten days of family leave per year for school events and caregiving. Contact Hélène Dubois or Yasmin El-Sayed in HR for questions.
